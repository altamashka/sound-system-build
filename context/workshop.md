## Workshop practices

Use inducted machine setups, guards/extraction, proper workholding and a scrap trial before final parts. Eye/hearing protection and suitable well-fitting FFP3/P3 wood-dust protection work alongside extraction. No gloves around rotating tools; finish-compatible gloves are for finishing away from machines. Never improvise freehand table-saw tapers, assume a jig fits every section, or let a blind pilot/fastener emerge through the show face.

Keep panels flat and supported. Check actual stock dimensions and condition before cutting or programming CAM. Rehearse glue-ups, label parts and cure times, arrange clamp/storage permission and clean up for the next member. Use product-specific finish/cure instructions and a fire-safe oily-applicator disposal method. Staff machine instruction is not structural design certification.

# Wood for the sound system

Price review: 23 October 2026. These figures are for planning; stock, delivery and VAT must be confirmed before ordering.

## What we need

Use **uncoated birch plywood**, nominally 12 mm and 18 mm, with BB/BB faces and a sound core. The cabinet shapes and the cutting files depend on the sheet thickness, so measure the actual boards before preparing the machine. BB/BB describes the surface; it does not confirm the core or glue quality. Check the manufacturer, ply construction, thickness tolerance and bonding specification. [Birch grading explained](https://www.wisaplywood.com/specifying-wisa/about-birch-plywood/)

The original designer files are not in this checkout. Review and simulate the actual layouts and make a test cut before cutting full sheets. The SKRAM's supplied 12 mm layout needs the 1250 mm sheet width; a 1220 mm sheet is not a direct replacement.

Cheaper pine, mixed-core ply and coated birch are different materials, not automatic substitutes for the planned birch. Keep any change to material or dimensions for a separate group decision and design check.

## Before ordering

1. Confirm workshop availability, price including VAT, sheet specification, storage, and whether outside sheets are allowed.
2. Compare matching delivered quotes, including cutting and unloading if needed. Check available sheets against the actual cutting layout.
3. Include spare material, test pieces and any parts that cannot fit in the offcuts. Agree the final spend before buying.

The workshop's listed 3 mm cutter has a 17 mm cutting length; it is **not automatically suitable** for cutting through 18 mm stock. Check the actual machine, cutter and toolpaths together.
